Knee pain can affect people of all ages. It may result from arthritis, sports injuries, ligament damage, meniscus tears or overuse. Early diagnosis and treatment help improve mobility and reduce pain.

Common Symptoms

  • Knee pain while walking
  • Knee swelling
  • Joint stiffness
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Clicking or locking of the knee
  • Instability while walking
  • Reduced range of motion
  • Difficulty standing after sitting

Common Causes

  • Osteoarthritis
  • Ligament injury (ACL/PCL/MCL/LCL)
  • Meniscus tear
  • Sports injury
  • Tendonitis
  • Bursitis
  • Gout
  • Fracture

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • X-ray Knee
  • MRI Knee (if required)
  • Blood Tests
  • Joint Fluid Analysis (selected cases)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can knee pain improve without surgery?

Yes. Many patients improve with medicines, weight management, exercises and physiotherapy. Surgery is required only for selected conditions.

Can arthritis cause knee pain?

Yes. Osteoarthritis is one of the most common causes of chronic knee pain, especially in older adults.

When should I consult a doctor?

Consult a doctor if pain persists, swelling increases, you cannot walk properly, or symptoms follow an injury.
