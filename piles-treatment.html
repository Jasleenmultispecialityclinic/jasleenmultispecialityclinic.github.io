Piles (hemorrhoids) are swollen veins in the rectum or anus that may cause pain, bleeding, itching or swelling. Early diagnosis and treatment help relieve symptoms and prevent complications.

Common Symptoms

  • Bleeding during bowel movements
  • Pain while passing stools
  • Itching around the anus
  • Swelling near the anus
  • Prolapse (lump coming out during stools)
  • Mucus discharge
  • Discomfort while sitting

Risk Factors

  • Chronic constipation
  • Low-fiber diet
  • Pregnancy
  • Obesity
  • Long periods of sitting
  • Heavy lifting
  • Chronic diarrhea

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • Digital Rectal Examination
  • Proctoscopy (if required)
  • Colonoscopy (selected patients)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can piles heal without surgery?

Early-stage piles often improve with medicines, dietary changes, hydration and lifestyle modification. Some advanced cases may require surgery.

Can constipation cause piles?

Yes. Straining during bowel movements and chronic constipation are common causes of piles.

How can I reduce my risk?

Eat a high-fiber diet, drink adequate water, avoid prolonged straining and stay physically active.
