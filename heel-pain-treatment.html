Heel pain is a common condition that can make walking difficult. It is often caused by plantar fasciitis, heel spurs, tendon injuries or prolonged standing. Early treatment helps relieve pain and restore normal walking.

Common Symptoms

  • Heel pain while walking
  • Pain with the first steps in the morning
  • Pain after prolonged standing
  • Tenderness under the heel
  • Swelling around the heel
  • Difficulty walking
  • Pain during exercise

Common Causes

  • Plantar Fasciitis
  • Heel Spur
  • Achilles Tendonitis
  • Overuse Injury
  • Obesity
  • Improper Footwear
  • Flat Feet

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • X-ray Foot
  • Ultrasound (if required)
  • MRI (selected cases)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most common cause of heel pain?

Plantar fasciitis is one of the most common causes of heel pain, especially in adults.

Can heel pain improve without surgery?

Yes. Most patients improve with medicines, stretching exercises, proper footwear and physiotherapy.

When should I consult a doctor?

Seek medical evaluation if heel pain is severe, persistent or prevents normal walking.
