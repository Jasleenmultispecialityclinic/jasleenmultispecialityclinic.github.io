What Is Antenatal Care (ANC)?

Antenatal care, also called ANC or prenatal care, includes medical checkups and guidance during pregnancy. Regular antenatal visits help monitor the mother's health, assess pregnancy progress, identify possible risk factors and plan appropriate investigations and care.

The timing and frequency of pregnancy visits may vary depending on gestational age, medical history and individual pregnancy needs.

When Should You Start Pregnancy Checkups?

Your first antenatal appointment should ideally be scheduled soon after pregnancy is confirmed. Early consultation helps review your medical history, current symptoms, medications, nutritional needs and the investigations recommended for your pregnancy.

Do not wait for symptoms to develop before scheduling routine pregnancy care.

Trimester-Wise Pregnancy Care

First Trimester

Early pregnancy visits focus on pregnancy confirmation, medical history, symptoms, appropriate investigations, nutrition, supplements and guidance regarding medicines and lifestyle during pregnancy.

Second Trimester

Follow-up visits help monitor maternal health, pregnancy progress, blood pressure, weight, routine investigations and fetal growth assessment as advised.

Third Trimester

Regular follow-up becomes important for monitoring maternal health, baby's movements, pregnancy progress, relevant investigations and discussing delivery planning.

Important Pregnancy Warning Signs

Seek prompt medical evaluation if you experience:

  • Vaginal bleeding during pregnancy
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • Severe headache or blurred vision
  • Sudden swelling of face or hands
  • Fluid leakage from the vagina
  • Reduced or absent fetal movements when movements are established
  • Persistent vomiting or inability to keep fluids down
  • Fever or significant illness during pregnancy

These symptoms may require urgent medical assessment. Do not delay care if you have concerning symptoms.

When should I get my first pregnancy checkup?

What is ANC in pregnancy?

ANC stands for Antenatal Care. It refers to medical checkups, monitoring and guidance provided during pregnancy to support the health of the mother and baby.

How often should I visit for pregnancy checkups?

The recommended visit schedule depends on the stage of pregnancy, medical history and individual risk factors. Your healthcare provider will guide you regarding follow-up visits.

Do I need pregnancy checkups if I feel completely fine?

Yes. Many pregnancy-related conditions may not cause noticeable symptoms initially. Routine antenatal visits help monitor your health and identify concerns when evaluation is needed.
