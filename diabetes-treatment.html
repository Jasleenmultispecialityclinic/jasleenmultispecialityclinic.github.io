Diabetes is a long-term condition that affects blood sugar levels. Early diagnosis, lifestyle changes, regular monitoring and appropriate treatment help prevent serious complications.

Common Symptoms

  • Frequent urination
  • Excessive thirst
  • Increased hunger
  • Weight loss
  • Blurred vision
  • Slow wound healing
  • Frequent infections
  • Fatigue

Diagnosis

  • Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS)
  • Post Prandial Blood Sugar (PPBS)
  • HbA1c
  • Urine Sugar
  • Kidney Function Tests

Frequently Asked Questions

Can diabetes be controlled?

Yes. With regular treatment, healthy eating, physical activity and monitoring, many people keep their blood sugar under good control.

Is HbA1c important?

HbA1c reflects the average blood sugar over the past 2–3 months and helps monitor diabetes control.
