Menopause is a natural stage in a woman's life when menstrual periods permanently stop. Hormonal changes during menopause can cause various physical and emotional symptoms. Early guidance and treatment can greatly improve quality of life.

Common Symptoms of Menopause

  • Hot flashes
  • Night sweats
  • Irregular periods
  • Vaginal dryness
  • Mood swings
  • Sleep disturbances
  • Weight gain
  • Reduced sexual desire
  • Joint and muscle pain
  • Fatigue

Causes of Menopause

  • Natural ageing
  • Removal of ovaries (surgical menopause)
  • Cancer treatment (chemotherapy/radiotherapy)
  • Premature ovarian insufficiency

Diagnosis & Evaluation

  • Clinical examination
  • Detailed menstrual history
  • Hormonal evaluation (if required)
  • Thyroid Function Test (TSH)
  • Blood Sugar Test
  • Bone Health Assessment (selected patients)

Treatment Options

Treatment depends on the severity of symptoms and your overall health. Lifestyle changes, diet modification, medications and individualized management plans can help improve menopausal symptoms and quality of life.

Frequently Asked Questions

At what age does menopause usually occur?

Most women experience menopause between 45 and 55 years of age, although it may occur earlier or later in some cases.

Can menopause cause weight gain?

Yes. Hormonal changes during menopause can slow metabolism and contribute to weight gain. A healthy diet and regular exercise can help.

Why does vaginal dryness occur during menopause?

Reduced estrogen levels can cause vaginal dryness, discomfort and pain during intercourse. Proper evaluation and treatment can improve symptoms.

Can menopause affect sleep and mood?

Yes. Hot flashes, night sweats and hormonal changes can lead to poor sleep, anxiety, irritability and mood swings.

When should I consult a doctor?

Consult a doctor if you have severe menopausal symptoms, abnormal bleeding after menopause, persistent pelvic pain or symptoms affecting your daily life.
