Sexual Health Consultation

Sexual health concerns are common and may affect people of different ages. Many problems can be related to stress, lifestyle, medications, hormonal conditions or other medical factors.

At Jasleen Multi-Speciality Clinic, patients can discuss their concerns privately and receive appropriate medical guidance and evaluation.

Common Concerns

  • Low sexual desire
  • Erectile difficulties
  • Premature ejaculation
  • Sexual performance concerns
  • Pain during sexual activity
  • Vaginal dryness
  • Sexual health concerns associated with stress
  • Concerns related to hormonal or medical conditions
  • Couples' sexual health concerns

When Should You Consult a Doctor?

If a sexual health problem is persistent, recurrent, causing distress, or affecting a relationship, professional medical evaluation can help identify possible underlying causes.

Confidential Consultation

Sexual health is a personal matter. Patients are encouraged to discuss their symptoms openly so that an appropriate history, examination or investigation can be considered when medically required.
