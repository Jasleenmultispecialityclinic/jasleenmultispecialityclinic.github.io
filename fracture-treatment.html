A fracture is a break or crack in a bone caused by trauma, falls, sports injuries or accidents. Early diagnosis and proper treatment help ensure correct bone healing and reduce long-term complications.

Common Symptoms

  • Severe pain after injury
  • Swelling around the injured area
  • Difficulty moving the limb
  • Visible deformity
  • Bruising
  • Tenderness
  • Inability to bear weight
  • Abnormal movement of the bone

Common Types of Fractures

  • Simple (Closed) Fracture
  • Open (Compound) Fracture
  • Hairline Fracture
  • Greenstick Fracture
  • Comminuted Fracture
  • Stress Fracture

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • X-ray
  • CT Scan (if required)
  • MRI (selected injuries)

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a fracture take to heal?

Most fractures heal within 6 to 12 weeks, depending on the type of fracture, age and overall health.

Will every fracture require surgery?

No. Many fractures can be treated successfully with a cast or splint. Surgery is required only for selected fractures.

Can I walk with a fracture?

Do not put weight on a suspected fracture until you have been examined by a doctor.
