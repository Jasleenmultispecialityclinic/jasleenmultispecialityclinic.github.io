Frequently Asked Questions

Which birth control method is best?

The best method depends on your age, health, medical history and future pregnancy plans.

Can I become pregnant immediately after stopping contraception?

Fertility returns quickly for many women, but the timing varies depending on the contraceptive method used.

Is family planning only for married couples?

Family planning counselling is available for anyone seeking advice on reproductive health and contraception.

Can PCOS affect pregnancy planning?

Yes. Women with PCOS may need additional counselling and treatment before planning pregnancy.

When should I consult a doctor?

Consult a doctor before starting, changing or stopping any contraceptive method, or if you are planning a pregnancy.
