Shoulder pain may result from muscle strain, tendon injuries, frozen shoulder, arthritis or rotator cuff disorders. Early diagnosis helps restore shoulder movement and reduce pain.

Common Symptoms

  • Shoulder pain
  • Difficulty lifting the arm
  • Shoulder stiffness
  • Pain while sleeping on the affected side
  • Clicking or popping sounds
  • Weakness in the shoulder
  • Reduced range of motion

Common Causes

  • Frozen Shoulder
  • Rotator Cuff Injury
  • Shoulder Arthritis
  • Tendonitis
  • Bursitis
  • Sports Injury
  • Shoulder Dislocation

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • X-ray Shoulder
  • Ultrasound (if indicated)
  • MRI Shoulder (selected cases)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can shoulder pain improve without surgery?

Yes. Many patients improve with medicines, exercises, physiotherapy and activity modification.

What is frozen shoulder?

Frozen shoulder is a condition that causes pain and progressive stiffness, limiting shoulder movement.

When should I consult a doctor?

Consult a doctor if shoulder pain persists, limits daily activities or follows an injury.
