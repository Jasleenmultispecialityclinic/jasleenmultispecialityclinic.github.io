A slipped (herniated) disc occurs when the soft inner part of a spinal disc pushes through its outer layer. It may press on nearby nerves, causing back pain, leg pain or numbness.

Common Symptoms

  • Severe lower back pain
  • Pain radiating to one or both legs
  • Numbness or tingling in the legs
  • Muscle weakness
  • Difficulty walking
  • Pain while bending or lifting
  • Neck pain with arm pain (cervical disc)

Common Causes

  • Age-related disc degeneration
  • Heavy lifting
  • Sudden twisting injury
  • Poor posture
  • Obesity
  • Repetitive physical work

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • Neurological Examination
  • X-ray Spine
  • MRI Spine (preferred test)
  • CT Scan (selected cases)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a slipped disc heal without surgery?

Yes. Many patients improve with medicines, activity modification, physiotherapy and exercises. Surgery is recommended only for selected cases.

Is MRI necessary?

MRI is the preferred imaging test when a slipped disc is suspected, especially if symptoms are severe or persistent.

When should I consult a doctor?

Consult a doctor if back pain radiates to the leg, causes numbness or weakness, or does not improve with conservative treatment.
