Common Symptoms

  • Pain starting around the navel and shifting to the right lower abdomen
  • Loss of appetite
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Fever
  • Pain while walking or coughing
  • Abdominal tenderness
  • Bloating

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • Complete Blood Count (CBC)
  • C-Reactive Protein (CRP)
  • Ultrasound Abdomen
  • CT Scan Abdomen (selected cases)
  • Urine Examination

Frequently Asked Questions

Can appendicitis go away without surgery?

Some selected cases may initially be treated with antibiotics, but surgery (appendectomy) remains the standard treatment for most patients with acute appendicitis.

What happens if appendicitis is left untreated?

The appendix can rupture, causing serious infection inside the abdomen. Early treatment reduces this risk.

How long is recovery after appendix surgery?

Recovery depends on the type of surgery and individual health, but many patients resume normal activities within a few weeks after medical clearance.
