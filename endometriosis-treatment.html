Endometriosis is a condition where tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside the uterus. It may cause severe pain, painful periods and difficulty conceiving.

Common Symptoms

  • Severe menstrual pain
  • Chronic pelvic pain
  • Pain during intercourse
  • Pain during bowel movement
  • Heavy menstrual bleeding
  • Difficulty conceiving
  • Fatigue

Diagnosis & Tests

  • Clinical Examination
  • Pelvic Ultrasound
  • MRI (if required)
  • Blood Tests
  • Laparoscopy (selected cases)

Treatment Options

Treatment depends on symptoms, age and pregnancy plans. Medical treatment, hormonal therapy, lifestyle modification and surgery may be advised when necessary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can endometriosis be cured?

It can be effectively managed with appropriate treatment and regular follow-up.

Can endometriosis cause infertility?

Yes. Some women may experience difficulty conceiving, but many achieve pregnancy with proper treatment.

When should I consult a doctor?

If you have severe period pain, chronic pelvic pain, painful intercourse or infertility, consult a doctor.
