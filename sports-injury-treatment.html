Sports injuries can affect muscles, ligaments, tendons, bones and joints. Prompt diagnosis and appropriate treatment help reduce recovery time and prevent long-term complications.

Common Sports Injuries

  • Ligament Sprain
  • Muscle Strain
  • Tendon Injury
  • ACL Injury
  • Meniscus Tear
  • Shoulder Dislocation
  • Ankle Sprain
  • Stress Fracture

Common Symptoms

  • Pain after injury
  • Swelling
  • Bruising
  • Difficulty walking
  • Joint instability
  • Reduced movement
  • Weakness

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • X-ray
  • MRI (if indicated)
  • Ultrasound (selected injuries)

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I continue playing after an injury?

No. Stop the activity immediately and seek medical evaluation to avoid worsening the injury.

Do all ligament injuries require surgery?

No. Many ligament injuries heal with rest, bracing, physiotherapy and rehabilitation. Surgery is needed only in selected cases.

When should I see a doctor?

Consult a doctor if pain, swelling or difficulty using the injured limb persists after an injury.
