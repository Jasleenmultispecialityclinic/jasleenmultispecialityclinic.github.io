Frequently Asked Questions

When should I visit a women's health clinic?

You should consult if you have irregular periods, severe menstrual pain, abnormal vaginal discharge, pregnancy-related concerns, infertility, PCOS symptoms or menopause-related problems.

Do you treat PCOS?

Yes. We provide consultation, lifestyle guidance and treatment planning for women with PCOS.

Do you provide pregnancy care?

Yes. We provide antenatal consultation, routine pregnancy check-ups and guidance throughout pregnancy.
