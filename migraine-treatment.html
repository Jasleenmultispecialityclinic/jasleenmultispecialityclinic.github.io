Migraine is a neurological condition that causes recurrent headaches, often associated with nausea, vomiting and sensitivity to light or sound.

Common Symptoms

  • Severe throbbing headache
  • Pain on one side of the head
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Sensitivity to light
  • Sensitivity to sound
  • Blurred vision
  • Dizziness
  • Aura before headache (in some patients)

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Evaluation
  • Neurological Examination
  • Blood Tests (if required)
  • CT Scan/MRI (selected patients)

Seek urgent medical care if you have a sudden severe headache, weakness, confusion, seizures or difficulty speaking.

Frequently Asked Questions

What triggers migraine?

Common triggers include stress, lack of sleep, certain foods, dehydration, hormonal changes and bright lights.

Can migraine be prevented?

Many patients benefit from identifying triggers, maintaining a healthy lifestyle and taking preventive medicines when recommended.

When should I consult a doctor?

If headaches become frequent, severe, interfere with daily activities or are associated with neurological symptoms, seek medical evaluation.
