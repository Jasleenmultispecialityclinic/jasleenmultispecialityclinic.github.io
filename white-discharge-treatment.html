White discharge (Leucorrhoea) is common in women. While a small amount of discharge may be normal, excessive discharge, itching, foul smell or burning may indicate an infection or hormonal imbalance. Early treatment helps prevent complications and improves quality of life.

Common Symptoms

  • Excessive white discharge
  • Bad smell
  • Vaginal itching
  • Burning sensation
  • Pelvic pain
  • Pain during intercourse
  • Frequent recurrence
  • Lower abdominal discomfort

Common Causes

  • Fungal infection (Candida)
  • Bacterial Vaginosis
  • Poor genital hygiene
  • Hormonal imbalance
  • Pregnancy
  • Diabetes
  • Low immunity
  • Sexually transmitted infections

Diagnosis & Tests

  • Clinical Examination
  • Vaginal pH Test
  • Microscopic Examination
  • Urine Test
  • Blood Sugar Test (if needed)

Treatment Options

Treatment depends on the underlying cause. Fungal, bacterial or hormonal causes require different treatment approaches. Maintaining proper hygiene and completing the prescribed treatment is important.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is white discharge normal?

A small amount of white discharge can be normal. However, excessive discharge with itching, burning or bad smell requires medical evaluation.

Can white discharge affect pregnancy?

Some infections can affect pregnancy if left untreated, so early treatment is recommended.

Can diabetes cause recurrent white discharge?

Yes. Poorly controlled diabetes increases the risk of recurrent fungal infections.

When should I consult a doctor?

If you have persistent discharge, bad smell, itching, pelvic pain or recurrent infection, consult a doctor.
