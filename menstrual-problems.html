Common Menstrual Problems

  • Irregular periods
  • Heavy menstrual bleeding
  • Painful periods (Dysmenorrhea)
  • Missed or delayed periods
  • Spotting between periods
  • Very frequent periods
  • Very light periods
  • Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS)

Common Causes

  • Hormonal imbalance
  • PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)
  • Thyroid disorders
  • Stress and anxiety
  • Sudden weight gain or weight loss
  • Pregnancy
  • Perimenopause
  • Certain medications

Diagnosis & Tests

  • Clinical examination
  • Pelvic Ultrasound
  • Thyroid Function Test (TSH)
  • Serum Prolactin
  • LH & FSH
  • Blood Sugar (FBS / HbA1c)
  • Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Treatment Options

Treatment depends on the underlying cause. Hormonal imbalance, PCOS, thyroid disorders and other conditions require different treatment approaches. Early diagnosis helps restore normal menstrual health.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are irregular periods normal?

Occasional changes may occur due to stress or lifestyle changes. Persistent irregular periods should be evaluated by a doctor.

Can thyroid problems affect periods?

Yes. Both hypothyroidism and hyperthyroidism can cause irregular, heavy or missed periods.

Can PCOS cause missed periods?

Yes. PCOS is one of the most common causes of irregular or absent menstrual cycles.

When should I consult a doctor?

If you have severe period pain, heavy bleeding, missed periods for more than 3 months, bleeding between periods or irregular cycles, consult a doctor.

Important: Persistent menstrual problems should not be ignored. Early diagnosis helps detect hormonal disorders, PCOS, thyroid disease and other conditions before complications develop.
