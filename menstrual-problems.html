Menstrual problems are common in women of all ages and may include irregular periods, heavy bleeding, painful menstruation or missed periods. Early diagnosis helps identify hormonal disorders, PCOS, thyroid problems and other underlying causes.

Common Menstrual Problems

  • Irregular periods
  • Heavy menstrual bleeding
  • Painful periods (Dysmenorrhea)
  • Missed or delayed periods
  • Spotting between periods
  • Very frequent periods
  • Very light periods
  • Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS)
