Menstrual Health

Changes in the timing, duration, amount of bleeding or severity of period pain can sometimes indicate an underlying medical condition.

Irregular periods may be associated with pregnancy, PCOS, thyroid disorders, changes in weight, stress, hormonal conditions or other gynecological causes.

PCOS is a common hormonal and metabolic condition that may affect menstrual cycles, ovulation, skin, hair and metabolic health.

Diagnosis should be based on appropriate clinical assessment rather than symptoms alone. Treatment and lifestyle recommendations are individualized.

Infertility Evaluation

Difficulty conceiving can have female factors, male factors, combined factors or sometimes no immediately identifiable cause.

A fertility evaluation may include reproductive history, ovulation assessment, selected laboratory tests, ultrasound and assessment of male fertility factors when clinically appropriate.

Vaginal Discharge & Women's Health

Vaginal discharge can be normal or may occur with infections, irritation or other gynecological conditions. Changes in colour, smell, amount, itching, burning or pain may require medical evaluation.

When Should You Consult a Doctor?

Consider a women's health consultation if you experience persistent changes in your periods, heavy bleeding, severe period pain, pelvic pain, abnormal vaginal discharge, fertility concerns or other symptoms that are affecting your health or daily life.

Urgent medical assessment may be needed for severe pelvic or abdominal pain, very heavy bleeding with dizziness or fainting, or other rapidly worsening symptoms.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a women's health checkup?

A women's health consultation is individualized and may include menstrual history, reproductive health, pregnancy planning, symptom assessment and evaluation of conditions such as PCOS, thyroid problems or other gynecological concerns.

When should a woman have a gynecology consultation?

A consultation may be useful for irregular periods, heavy or painful bleeding, abnormal vaginal discharge, pelvic pain, fertility concerns, pregnancy planning or other reproductive health concerns.

Can PCOS be checked during a women's health consultation?

Yes. If symptoms or medical history suggest PCOS, a healthcare professional can assess menstrual history, symptoms, metabolic factors and other relevant findings.

Can I consult for pregnancy planning?

Yes. Preconception consultation can help review medical history, medications, lifestyle, nutritional needs and other factors relevant to a healthy pregnancy.
