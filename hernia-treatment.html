A hernia occurs when an internal organ or tissue pushes through a weak area in the abdominal wall or surrounding muscle. Early diagnosis and timely treatment help prevent complications such as obstruction or strangulation.

Common Symptoms

  • Visible swelling or lump in the groin or abdomen
  • Pain while lifting heavy objects
  • Groin discomfort
  • Burning or dragging sensation
  • Swelling that increases on coughing
  • Feeling of heaviness
  • Pain after prolonged standing

Types of Hernia

  • Inguinal Hernia
  • Umbilical Hernia
  • Femoral Hernia
  • Incisional Hernia
  • Epigastric Hernia

Diagnosis

Emergency: Seek immediate medical attention if the hernia becomes painful, cannot be pushed back, changes colour, or is associated with vomiting or severe abdominal pain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a hernia heal without surgery?

No. A hernia usually does not heal on its own. Surgery is the definitive treatment in most cases.

Is hernia surgery safe?

Yes. Hernia repair is a commonly performed procedure with good outcomes when done by an experienced surgeon.

Can I exercise with a hernia?

Avoid heavy lifting and strenuous exercise until you have been evaluated by a doctor.

Can a hernia come back after surgery?

Recurrence is uncommon but can occur. Following your surgeon's advice and avoiding heavy lifting during recovery reduces the risk.
