Back pain is one of the most common musculoskeletal problems. It may occur due to muscle strain, poor posture, degenerative spine disease or disc-related conditions. Early diagnosis and treatment can improve pain and mobility.

Common Symptoms

  • Lower back pain
  • Upper back pain
  • Pain while bending
  • Muscle stiffness
  • Pain radiating to the legs
  • Numbness or tingling
  • Difficulty walking
  • Reduced spinal movement

Common Causes

  • Muscle strain
  • Poor posture
  • Slip disc
  • Sciatica
  • Arthritis of the spine
  • Osteoporosis
  • Heavy lifting injuries

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • X-ray Spine
  • MRI Spine (when indicated)
  • Neurological Examination
  • Blood Tests (if required)

Emergency: Seek immediate medical attention if back pain is associated with leg weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, severe trauma or numbness around the groin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can back pain improve without surgery?

Yes. Many cases improve with medicines, activity modification, exercises and physiotherapy. Surgery is only required for selected conditions.

Can prolonged sitting cause back pain?

Yes. Poor posture and prolonged sitting can contribute to back pain and muscle stiffness.

When should I consult a doctor?

Consult a doctor if pain lasts more than a few weeks, is severe, follows an injury, or is associated with numbness, weakness or difficulty walking.
