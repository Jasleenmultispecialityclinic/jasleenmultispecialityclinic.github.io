Asthma is a chronic disease that causes inflammation and narrowing of the airways. Proper treatment and regular follow-up help control symptoms and reduce asthma attacks.

Common Symptoms

  • Shortness of breath
  • Wheezing
  • Chest tightness
  • Persistent cough
  • Cough at night or early morning
  • Difficulty breathing during exercise
  • Frequent chest infections

Common Triggers

  • Dust
  • Smoke
  • Pollen
  • Cold weather
  • Respiratory infections
  • Exercise
  • Strong perfumes or chemicals

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • Oxygen Saturation
  • Peak Flow Measurement
  • Spirometry (if available)
  • Chest X-ray (when indicated)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can asthma be cured?

Asthma is a long-term condition, but with proper treatment and regular follow-up, most people can control their symptoms effectively.

Can children develop asthma?

Yes. Asthma can occur in both children and adults.

Is an inhaler safe?

Yes. When used correctly and as prescribed, inhalers are safe and effective for asthma control.
