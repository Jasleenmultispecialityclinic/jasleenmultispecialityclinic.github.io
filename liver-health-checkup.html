Medical evaluation and guidance for liver health, abnormal liver tests and common liver-related concerns.

Why Is Liver Health Important?

The liver performs many important functions, including processing nutrients, producing proteins and helping the body process medicines and other substances. Maintaining liver health is an important part of overall healthcare.

Common Liver-Related Conditions

  • Fatty liver disease
  • Hepatitis and other liver inflammation
  • Abnormal liver function tests
  • Liver-related problems associated with metabolic conditions
  • Alcohol-related liver problems

Risk Factors for Liver Problems

  • Overweight or obesity
  • Diabetes
  • High cholesterol and triglycerides
  • Excessive alcohol consumption
  • Certain medicines
  • Viral hepatitis
  • Some metabolic and inherited conditions

Fatty Liver

Fatty liver occurs when excess fat accumulates in the liver. It may be associated with obesity, diabetes, abnormal cholesterol levels and other metabolic risk factors. Some people have no symptoms and discover fatty liver during an ultrasound or medical evaluation.

Possible Symptoms

Many liver conditions may not cause symptoms in the early stages. Depending on the condition, symptoms can include tiredness, abdominal discomfort, loss of appetite, nausea, jaundice or other changes. Persistent or concerning symptoms should be medically evaluated.

Liver Function Tests

Depending on the clinical situation, a doctor may recommend blood tests to assess liver function and related parameters. The exact tests required depend on symptoms, medical history, examination and previous results.

Ultrasound and Other Investigations

When clinically appropriate, abdominal ultrasound or other imaging may be recommended to evaluate the liver and surrounding organs. Further investigations may be advised depending on the findings.

How to Support Liver Health

  • Maintain a healthy body weight.
  • Follow a balanced diet.
  • Stay physically active.
  • Avoid excessive alcohol consumption.
  • Take medicines only as advised.
  • Control diabetes and cholesterol when applicable.
  • Discuss long-term use of medicines or supplements with your doctor.

Liver Health and Diabetes

Diabetes and metabolic conditions can be associated with fatty liver. Good blood sugar control, healthy eating and appropriate weight management can form an important part of overall metabolic health.

Liver Health and Cholesterol

Abnormal cholesterol and triglyceride levels can occur along with metabolic conditions associated with fatty liver. Managing overall cardiovascular and metabolic health may be beneficial.

When Should You Consult a Doctor?

  • Abnormal liver function test results
  • Fatty liver reported on ultrasound
  • Persistent abdominal discomfort
  • Yellowing of the eyes or skin
  • Unexplained persistent fatigue
  • Known hepatitis or other liver disease
  • Concern about liver health or long-term medication use
