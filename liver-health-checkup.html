Liver Health Checkup & Consultation

The liver performs many important functions, including processing nutrients, producing proteins and helping the body process medicines and other substances. Liver problems can sometimes develop without obvious symptoms.

At Jasleen Multi-Speciality Clinic in Behram, patients can discuss abnormal liver reports, fatty liver, metabolic risk factors and other liver-related concerns with a qualified healthcare professional.

Who May Need Liver Health Assessment?

  • People with abnormal liver blood tests
  • People with fatty liver detected on ultrasound
  • People with diabetes or metabolic risk factors
  • People who are overweight or have obesity
  • People with a history of liver disease
  • People with certain medication-related liver concerns
  • People with symptoms or findings that require medical evaluation

Common Liver Function Tests

The tests required depend on the patient's symptoms, medical history and clinical findings. Your doctor may consider blood tests such as:

  • ALT
  • AST
  • Alkaline phosphatase
  • Bilirubin
  • Albumin
  • Total protein

Other blood tests, imaging or specialist evaluation may be recommended when clinically appropriate.

What Is Fatty Liver?

Fatty liver means that excess fat has accumulated in the liver. It is frequently associated with metabolic conditions such as obesity, diabetes and abnormal blood lipids, although there are different possible causes.

A fatty liver finding does not by itself determine the severity of liver disease. Your doctor may consider your medical history, blood tests, metabolic risk factors and imaging findings when deciding on further evaluation.

Healthy Lifestyle for Liver Health

  • Maintain a healthy body weight where appropriate.
  • Stay physically active.
  • Follow a balanced eating pattern.
  • Manage diabetes and blood pressure.
  • Address abnormal cholesterol and triglycerides.
  • Avoid tobacco.
  • Discuss alcohol use honestly with your healthcare professional.
  • Avoid unnecessary medicines or supplements without medical advice.

When Should You Consult a Doctor?

Medical evaluation may be appropriate if you have persistently abnormal liver tests, fatty liver on imaging, unexplained jaundice, persistent abdominal symptoms, unusual tiredness or other concerning findings.

Seek urgent medical attention for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, especially jaundice with significant illness, confusion, vomiting blood, black stools or severe abdominal pain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a liver health checkup important?

Some liver conditions may develop with few or no symptoms. Assessment can be useful when liver tests are abnormal or when there are risk factors such as obesity, diabetes or previous liver disease.

Which tests are used to assess liver health?

Depending on the clinical situation, your doctor may recommend liver blood tests such as ALT, AST, bilirubin and albumin, along with other blood tests or imaging when appropriate.

What is fatty liver?

Fatty liver refers to excess fat accumulation in the liver. It can be associated with metabolic risk factors such as obesity, diabetes and abnormal blood lipids, although causes can vary.

Can lifestyle changes help fatty liver?

Healthy eating, regular physical activity, appropriate weight management and management of metabolic conditions can support liver and overall health.
