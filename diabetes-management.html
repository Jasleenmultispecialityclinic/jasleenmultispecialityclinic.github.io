Diabetes Management & Care

Diabetes is a long-term metabolic condition that affects how the body regulates blood glucose. Regular monitoring, healthy lifestyle habits, appropriate medical treatment and follow-up are important parts of diabetes care.

At Jasleen Multi-Speciality Clinic in Behram, patients can discuss blood sugar reports, diabetes symptoms, lifestyle factors, treatment plans and regular follow-up with a qualified healthcare professional.

Common Types of Diabetes

  • Type 1 diabetes: A condition in which the body produces little or no insulin and requires ongoing medical management.
  • Type 2 diabetes: The most common form of diabetes, often associated with insulin resistance and other metabolic risk factors.
  • Gestational diabetes: Diabetes diagnosed during pregnancy and requiring appropriate pregnancy and medical follow-up.

Common Symptoms of High Blood Sugar

  • Increased thirst
  • Frequent urination
  • Increased hunger
  • Unexplained weight changes
  • Tiredness or weakness
  • Blurred vision
  • Slow healing of wounds
  • Recurrent infections

Some people with diabetes may have few or no noticeable symptoms. Routine testing can therefore be important for people with risk factors.

Diabetes Tests

Your doctor may recommend blood glucose testing based on your symptoms, medical history and risk factors.

  • Fasting Blood Glucose
  • HbA1c
  • Random Blood Glucose
  • Post-meal glucose testing when appropriate

The interpretation of a glucose result depends on the type of test, timing and individual clinical circumstances.

Diabetes Lifestyle Management

Lifestyle management is an important part of diabetes care. A balanced diet, regular physical activity, maintaining a healthy weight, adequate sleep and avoiding tobacco can support overall metabolic health.

  • Choose a balanced eating pattern.
  • Include vegetables, whole grains and appropriate protein sources.
  • Limit excess added sugars and highly processed foods.
  • Stay physically active as advised by your healthcare professional.
  • Maintain a healthy body weight where appropriate.
  • Take prescribed medicines regularly.
  • Attend scheduled follow-up visits.

Diabetes Monitoring

Regular monitoring can help your healthcare professional understand how well diabetes is being controlled and whether your treatment plan needs adjustment.

Depending on your individual situation, your doctor may also monitor blood pressure, cholesterol, kidney function, eye health, foot health and other diabetes-related risk factors.

Diabetes and Blood Pressure

Diabetes and high blood pressure commonly occur together and can increase cardiovascular and kidney-related risks. Regular blood pressure monitoring and appropriate treatment are therefore important for people with diabetes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is diabetes?

Diabetes is a chronic metabolic condition in which blood glucose levels remain higher than normal. Proper evaluation, monitoring, lifestyle management and medical treatment can help reduce the risk of complications.

What tests are used to check diabetes?

Common tests include fasting blood glucose and HbA1c. Your healthcare professional may recommend other tests depending on your symptoms and medical history.

Can lifestyle changes help manage diabetes?

Yes. Healthy eating, regular physical activity, maintaining a healthy weight, avoiding tobacco and following the prescribed treatment plan can support diabetes management.

Should diabetes medicines be stopped when blood sugar improves?

Do not stop or change diabetes medicines without discussing the decision with your healthcare professional.
