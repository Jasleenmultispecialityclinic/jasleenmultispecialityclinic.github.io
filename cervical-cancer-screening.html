Cervical Cancer Screening

Regular cervical cancer screening helps detect abnormal cervical changes before cancer develops.

Who Needs Screening?

  • Women aged 21–65 years
  • Persistent vaginal discharge
  • Abnormal bleeding
  • Positive HPV history

Benefits

  • Early diagnosis
  • Reduced cancer risk
  • Simple outpatient procedure

FAQ

Is cervical cancer preventable?

Regular screening helps detect precancerous changes early.

Which test is used?

Pap Smear is the most common screening test.
