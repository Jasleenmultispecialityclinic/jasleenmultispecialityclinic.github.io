Pap Smear Test

Early detection of cervical cancer through Pap Smear screening.

Pap Smear is a simple screening test used to detect abnormal cervical cells before they develop into cervical cancer.

Who Should Get a Pap Smear?

  • Women aged 21–65 years
  • Married women
  • Women with abnormal vaginal bleeding
  • Women with persistent vaginal discharge
  • Women with HPV infection risk

Benefits

  • Early cancer detection
  • Detection of precancerous changes
  • Simple & painless procedure
  • Quick outpatient test

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pap Smear painful?

Usually no. Most women experience only mild discomfort.

How often should I get a Pap Smear?

Your doctor will advise the screening interval based on your age and medical history.

Can Pap Smear detect cervical cancer?

It helps detect abnormal cervical cells early, allowing treatment before cancer develops.
