Sciatica occurs when the sciatic nerve becomes irritated or compressed, usually due to a slipped disc or spinal degeneration. It commonly causes pain that travels from the lower back to the buttock and down the leg.

Common Symptoms

  • Lower back pain
  • Pain radiating to one leg
  • Burning sensation in the leg
  • Numbness or tingling
  • Muscle weakness
  • Pain while sitting
  • Difficulty walking
  • Reduced leg strength

Common Causes

  • Slip Disc (Herniated Disc)
  • Lumbar Spondylosis
  • Spinal Canal Stenosis
  • Degenerative Disc Disease
  • Trauma
  • Piriformis Syndrome

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • Neurological Examination
  • X-ray Spine
  • MRI Lumbar Spine
  • CT Scan (selected patients)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can sciatica go away without surgery?

Yes. Most patients improve with medicines, physiotherapy, exercises and lifestyle modifications. Surgery is needed only in selected cases.

Is walking good for sciatica?

Gentle walking is often helpful, but activities should be tailored to your symptoms and doctor's advice.

When should I see a doctor?

Consult a doctor if pain lasts more than a few weeks, becomes severe, or is associated with weakness or numbness.
