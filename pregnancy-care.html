Frequently Asked Questions

When should I visit a doctor after a positive pregnancy test?

It is recommended to schedule your first antenatal visit as soon as pregnancy is confirmed so that appropriate tests, supplements and guidance can be started early.

How often are pregnancy checkups needed?

The frequency of visits depends on the stage of pregnancy and your individual health. Your doctor will advise a suitable follow-up schedule.

What warning signs need immediate medical attention?

Heavy bleeding, severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, severe headache, reduced fetal movements in later pregnancy or leaking of fluid should be evaluated by a doctor without delay.
