Pregnancy Care

Healthy pregnancy begins with regular antenatal checkups and timely medical care. At Jasleen Multi-Speciality Clinic, we provide pregnancy consultations, routine antenatal care, nutritional guidance, blood pressure monitoring, diabetes screening, fetal well-being assessment and counseling throughout pregnancy.

Our Pregnancy Care Services

  • Pregnancy confirmation
  • Regular ANC checkups
  • Blood pressure monitoring
  • Gestational diabetes screening
  • Diet and nutrition counseling
  • High-risk pregnancy guidance
  • Routine laboratory investigations
  • Pregnancy counseling

When Should You Visit?

If you have missed your period, a positive pregnancy test or are already pregnant, early consultation helps ensure the best possible care for both mother and baby.
