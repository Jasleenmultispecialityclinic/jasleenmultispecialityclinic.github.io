Infertility Treatment

Infertility means difficulty in achieving pregnancy despite regular unprotected intercourse for an appropriate period. Both female and male factors can contribute. At Jasleen Multi-Speciality Clinic, we provide evaluation, counseling and personalized treatment plans to help couples understand possible causes and available treatment options.

Common Causes of Infertility

  • PCOS and ovulation disorders
  • Blocked fallopian tubes
  • Hormonal imbalance
  • Endometriosis
  • Male factor infertility
  • Thyroid disorders
  • Age-related fertility decline

Our Services

We provide fertility assessment, menstrual cycle evaluation, ovulation guidance, lifestyle counseling, pregnancy planning and referral for advanced fertility procedures whenever required.

When Should You Seek Medical Advice?

Couples who are concerned about difficulty conceiving, irregular ovulation, recurrent pregnancy loss or known reproductive health conditions should seek medical evaluation for individualized guidance.
