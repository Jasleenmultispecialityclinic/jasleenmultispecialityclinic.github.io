Frequently Asked Questions

When should a couple seek infertility evaluation?

Couples who have been trying to conceive without success should consult a doctor for evaluation based on their age, medical history and individual circumstances.

Can PCOS cause infertility?

Yes. PCOS can affect ovulation and make conception more difficult for some women. Appropriate evaluation and treatment may improve the chances of pregnancy.

Is infertility only a female problem?

No. Infertility may be related to female factors, male factors or a combination of both. Evaluation of both partners is often recommended.
