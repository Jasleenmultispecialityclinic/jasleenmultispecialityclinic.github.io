Frequently Asked Questions

When should I schedule my first pregnancy visit?

Schedule your first antenatal visit as soon as your pregnancy is confirmed so that appropriate evaluation and routine care can begin early.

How often are ANC checkups needed?

The number of antenatal visits depends on the stage of pregnancy and your individual health needs. Your doctor will advise an appropriate schedule.

Do you provide pregnancy counselling?

Yes. We provide counselling on nutrition, lifestyle, routine investigations and maternal health throughout pregnancy.
