When should I see an orthopedic doctor?

Consult an orthopedic doctor if you have persistent joint pain, fractures, sports injuries, back pain, neck pain or difficulty walking.

Do all fractures require surgery?

No. Some fractures heal with proper immobilization, while others may require surgical management depending on the injury.
