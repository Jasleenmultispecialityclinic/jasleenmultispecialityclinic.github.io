Acidity and gastritis are common digestive problems that may cause burning in the chest, stomach pain, bloating and indigestion. Early treatment helps prevent complications and improves quality of life.

Common Symptoms

  • Heartburn
  • Burning sensation in the chest
  • Upper abdominal pain
  • Acid reflux
  • Bloating
  • Nausea
  • Indigestion
  • Belching

Common Causes

  • Irregular eating habits
  • Spicy or oily food
  • Stress
  • Excess tea or coffee
  • Smoking or alcohol
  • Certain medicines (such as some painkillers)
  • Helicobacter pylori infection

Investigations

  • Clinical Examination
  • Blood Tests (if required)
  • H. pylori Testing (selected patients)
  • Upper GI Endoscopy (when indicated)

Seek urgent medical attention if you have severe abdominal pain, vomiting blood, black stools or difficulty swallowing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can acidity be cured?

Many people improve with diet changes, healthy habits and appropriate medicines. The treatment depends on the underlying cause.

What foods should I avoid?

Spicy foods, very oily meals, excess caffeine and late-night meals can worsen symptoms in some people.

When should I consult a doctor?

If symptoms persist, recur frequently, or are associated with weight loss, vomiting, black stools or severe pain, seek medical evaluation.
