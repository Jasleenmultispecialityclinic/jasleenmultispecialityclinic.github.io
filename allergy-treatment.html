Allergies occur when the immune system reacts to substances such as dust, pollen, foods, medicines or insect bites. Early diagnosis and appropriate treatment can help control symptoms and improve quality of life.

Common Symptoms

  • Sneezing
  • Runny or blocked nose
  • Itchy eyes
  • Skin rash
  • Hives (Urticaria)
  • Itching
  • Swelling of lips or face
  • Wheezing or breathing difficulty

Common Allergy Triggers

  • Dust mites
  • Pollen
  • Pet dander
  • Certain foods
  • Medicines
  • Insect bites
  • Mold
  • Smoke

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • Detailed Allergy History
  • Blood Tests (when required)
  • Referral for Allergy Testing (selected cases)

Emergency: Seek immediate medical attention if you develop difficulty breathing, swelling of the tongue or throat, or severe allergic reactions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can allergies be cured?

Many allergies can be effectively controlled by avoiding triggers and using appropriate treatment prescribed by a doctor.

Can allergies cause asthma?

Yes. Allergies can trigger or worsen asthma symptoms in some individuals.

When should I consult a doctor?

Consult a doctor if allergy symptoms are severe, recurrent or interfere with your daily activities.
