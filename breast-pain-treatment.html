Breast pain (mastalgia) is a common problem in women. It may be related to hormonal changes, infections, breastfeeding, cysts or other breast conditions. Early medical evaluation helps identify the cause and provide appropriate treatment.

Common Symptoms of Breast Pain

  • Pain in one or both breasts
  • Breast tenderness
  • Swelling or heaviness
  • Burning or sharp pain
  • Pain before or during periods
  • Pain during breastfeeding
  • Breast lump with pain (in some cases)
  • Redness or warmth of the breast

Common Causes

  • Hormonal changes during the menstrual cycle
  • Fibrocystic breast changes
  • Breastfeeding-related problems
  • Mastitis (breast infection)
  • Breast cysts
  • Muscle or chest wall pain
  • Injury to the breast
  • Certain medications

Diagnosis & Tests

  • Clinical breast examination
  • Breast Ultrasound
  • Mammography (when indicated)
  • FNAC/Biopsy (selected cases)
  • Hormonal evaluation (if required)
  • Blood investigations

Treatment Options

Treatment depends on the underlying cause. Hormonal breast pain, infections, cysts and breastfeeding-related conditions require different treatment approaches. Early evaluation helps rule out serious conditions and provides effective symptom relief.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is breast pain always a sign of breast cancer?

No. Most cases of breast pain are not caused by breast cancer. Hormonal changes, infections, cysts and muscle strain are much more common causes. However, persistent symptoms should always be evaluated.

When should I see a doctor for breast pain?

Consult a doctor if breast pain lasts for more than a few weeks, is severe, is associated with a lump, nipple discharge, redness, swelling or fever.

Can hormonal changes cause breast pain?

Yes. Hormonal fluctuations before or during menstrual periods are one of the most common causes of breast pain and tenderness.

Can breastfeeding cause breast pain?

Yes. Blocked milk ducts, mastitis, nipple injury and improper breastfeeding technique can all cause breast pain.

Can breast cysts cause pain?

Yes. Some breast cysts can cause pain, tenderness or a feeling of heaviness, especially before menstruation.
