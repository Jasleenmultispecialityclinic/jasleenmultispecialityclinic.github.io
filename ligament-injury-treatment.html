Ligament Injury Treatment in Behram

Medical evaluation and guidance for ligament injuries affecting the knee, ankle, shoulder and other joints.

What Is a Ligament Injury?

Ligaments are strong bands of tissue that connect bones and help stabilize joints. A ligament injury can occur when a joint is suddenly twisted, overstretched or subjected to excessive force.

Common Causes

  • Sports injuries
  • Falls and accidents
  • Sudden twisting of a joint
  • Overstretching
  • Direct injury to a joint

Common Symptoms

  • Pain around the affected joint
  • Swelling
  • Tenderness
  • Difficulty putting weight on the affected limb
  • Reduced joint movement
  • Feeling of instability in the joint
  • Bruising in some injuries

Ligament Injuries We Evaluate

  • Knee ligament injuries
  • Ankle ligament injuries
  • Shoulder ligament injuries
  • Sports-related ligament injuries
  • Sprains and joint injuries

Diagnosis

Diagnosis depends on the location and severity of the injury. A doctor may perform a physical examination and, when clinically indicated, recommend imaging such as an X-ray, ultrasound or MRI.

Treatment

Treatment depends on the type and severity of the injury. Depending on the clinical assessment, management may include rest, activity modification, ice, supportive measures, pain management, physiotherapy or referral to an orthopedic specialist when required.

When Should You See a Doctor?

Medical evaluation is recommended when there is significant swelling, severe pain, difficulty walking or putting weight on the limb, joint instability, deformity, or symptoms that do not improve.
