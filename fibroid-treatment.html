Common Symptoms of Fibroids

  • Heavy menstrual bleeding
  • Pelvic pain or pressure
  • Frequent urination
  • Lower back pain
  • Pain during intercourse
  • Constipation
  • Difficulty conceiving

Diagnosis & Tests

  • Pelvic Examination
  • Ultrasound Scan (USG)
  • MRI (if required)
  • Blood Tests
  • Pregnancy Test (if needed)

Treatment Options

Treatment depends on the size, number and symptoms of fibroids. Many women can be treated successfully with medicines, lifestyle changes and regular follow-up, while some cases may require surgery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can fibroids be treated without surgery?

Yes. Many fibroids can be managed with medicines and regular monitoring depending on symptoms and size.

Can fibroids affect pregnancy?

Some fibroids may interfere with fertility or pregnancy, but many women have healthy pregnancies with proper care.

When should I consult a doctor?

If you have heavy bleeding, severe pelvic pain, frequent urination or difficulty conceiving, consult a doctor.
