Frequently Asked Questions

When should I consult an orthopaedic doctor?

If you have persistent bone, joint, muscle or spine pain, swelling, restricted movement or an injury after a fall or accident, you should seek medical evaluation.

Do all fractures require surgery?

No. Many fractures can be managed with appropriate immobilization, while some may require surgical treatment depending on the type and severity.

Can back pain be treated without surgery?

Many patients improve with medicines, physiotherapy, exercises and lifestyle modification. Surgery is required only in selected cases.
