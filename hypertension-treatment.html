High blood pressure often has no symptoms but can increase the risk of heart attack, stroke and kidney disease if left untreated. Regular monitoring and treatment are essential.

Common Symptoms

  • Headache
  • Dizziness
  • Blurred vision
  • Chest discomfort
  • Shortness of breath
  • Nose bleeding (occasionally)
  • Often no symptoms

Diagnosis

  • Blood Pressure Measurement
  • ECG
  • Blood Sugar
  • Kidney Function Tests
  • Lipid Profile
  • Urine Examination

Frequently Asked Questions

Can high blood pressure be cured?

Hypertension usually requires long-term management. Healthy lifestyle habits and prescribed medicines help keep blood pressure under control.

Can hypertension damage the heart?

Yes. Uncontrolled hypertension increases the risk of heart disease, stroke, kidney disease and eye problems.

How often should I check my BP?

Your doctor will advise the monitoring schedule based on your blood pressure readings and overall health.
