Ovarian cysts are fluid-filled sacs that develop on or inside the ovaries. Many cysts disappear naturally, but some require medical evaluation and treatment, especially if they cause pain or menstrual irregularities.

Common Symptoms of Ovarian Cysts

  • Pelvic pain
  • Lower abdominal pain
  • Irregular menstrual periods
  • Pain during intercourse
  • Bloating or abdominal fullness
  • Frequent urination
  • Difficulty in conceiving (some cases)
  • Sudden severe pain (medical emergency)

Common Causes

  • Hormonal imbalance
  • PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)
  • Endometriosis
  • Pregnancy-related cysts
  • Functional ovarian cysts
  • Corpus luteum cyst

Diagnosis & Tests

  • Clinical examination
  • Pelvic Ultrasound (USG)
  • Pregnancy Test
  • Hormonal Profile (if required)
  • CA-125 Test (selected cases)
  • MRI/CT Scan (if indicated)

Treatment Options

Treatment depends on the type, size and symptoms of the ovarian cyst. Many cysts resolve naturally, while others may need medicines, regular ultrasound follow-up or surgery in selected cases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an ovarian cyst disappear on its own?

Yes. Many functional ovarian cysts disappear naturally within a few menstrual cycles. Regular follow-up may be advised.

Can ovarian cysts affect pregnancy?

Most ovarian cysts do not affect pregnancy, but some types may require monitoring or treatment.

Can ovarian cysts cause infertility?

Some ovarian cysts, especially those related to endometriosis or PCOS, may affect fertility. Early evaluation helps in proper management.

Do all ovarian cysts require surgery?

No. Many cysts can be managed with observation, medicines and ultrasound follow-up. Surgery is recommended only in selected cases.

When should I consult a doctor?

Consult a doctor if you have persistent pelvic pain, irregular periods, abdominal swelling, severe pain or sudden onset of symptoms.
