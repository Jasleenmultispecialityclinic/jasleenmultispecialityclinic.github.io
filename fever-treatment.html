Fever is a symptom of many illnesses including viral infections, bacterial infections, dengue, typhoid and malaria. Proper diagnosis helps ensure timely and appropriate treatment.

Common Symptoms

  • High body temperature
  • Chills and shivering
  • Headache
  • Body ache
  • Weakness
  • Sore throat
  • Cough
  • Loss of appetite

Investigations

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC)
  • Dengue NS1 / IgM
  • Typhoid Test
  • Malaria Test
  • Urine Examination
  • Chest X-ray (if indicated)

Seek urgent medical attention if you have persistent high fever, difficulty breathing, confusion, severe dehydration, seizures or fever lasting more than 3 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I see a doctor for fever?

Consult a doctor if fever is high, lasts more than 2–3 days, or is associated with severe symptoms such as breathing difficulty, persistent vomiting, confusion or severe weakness.

Can viral fever be treated with antibiotics?

No. Antibiotics are only effective against bacterial infections and should be taken only when prescribed by a doctor.

Should I drink more fluids during fever?

Yes. Adequate hydration helps prevent dehydration during fever.
