Infertility Evaluation in Behram

Fertility evaluation and reproductive health guidance for couples having difficulty conceiving.

What Is Infertility?

Infertility refers to difficulty achieving pregnancy despite regular unprotected sexual intercourse over an appropriate period. Fertility problems can affect either partner, and sometimes no single cause is identified.

When Should You Seek a Fertility Evaluation?

A fertility evaluation may be considered when pregnancy has not occurred despite regular unprotected intercourse, or earlier when there are factors such as irregular or absent periods, known reproductive problems, previous pelvic conditions or other concerns.

Common Causes of Female Infertility

  • Ovulation problems
  • PCOS / PCOD
  • Endometriosis
  • Problems involving the fallopian tubes
  • Uterine conditions such as fibroids
  • Hormonal disorders
  • Age-related reduction in fertility

Male Factors Can Also Affect Fertility

Fertility problems can involve the male partner as well. Depending on the clinical history, evaluation may include assessment of medical history, lifestyle factors and semen analysis when indicated.

What May Be Included in an Infertility Evaluation?

The evaluation is individualized. Depending on the history and clinical findings, a doctor may consider menstrual and ovulation history, medical and surgical history, physical examination and appropriate investigations.

  • Pregnancy history and menstrual history
  • Ovulation assessment when indicated
  • Hormonal investigations when clinically appropriate
  • Pelvic ultrasound when required
  • Assessment of uterine or ovarian conditions
  • Male partner evaluation when indicated

Infertility and PCOS

PCOS can affect ovulation and may make conception more difficult for some women. Proper evaluation can help identify whether irregular ovulation is contributing to difficulty conceiving.

Infertility and Endometriosis

Endometriosis may affect fertility in some women. If symptoms or medical history suggest endometriosis, appropriate evaluation can help guide further management.

Treatment Depends on the Cause

There is no single treatment suitable for every couple. Management depends on the underlying cause, age, duration of infertility, previous pregnancy history and other medical factors. Treatment may include lifestyle measures, medicines, correction of specific conditions or referral for fertility treatment when appropriate.
