Infertility Evaluation & Fertility Consultation

Difficulty conceiving can have many possible causes. Fertility factors may involve the female partner, male partner, both partners or sometimes remain unexplained. A structured evaluation helps identify factors that may be affecting conception and guides further management.

At Jasleen Multi-Speciality Clinic in Behram, fertility consultation begins with a detailed medical and reproductive history followed by appropriate clinical assessment and investigations when required.

When Should You Consider an Infertility Evaluation?

Infertility evaluation may be considered when a couple has difficulty conceiving despite regular unprotected intercourse. Earlier assessment may also be appropriate when there are known fertility concerns or risk factors.

  • Irregular or absent menstrual periods
  • Known ovulation problems
  • Previous pelvic or reproductive problems
  • History of certain reproductive surgeries or infections
  • Known male fertility concerns
  • Difficulty conceiving despite trying for an appropriate period

Female Fertility Evaluation

Female fertility assessment may include a review of menstrual cycles, ovulation patterns, previous pregnancies, medical history and other reproductive factors.

Depending on the clinical situation, the doctor may recommend investigations such as pelvic ultrasound or selected blood tests to assess ovulation and hormonal factors.

Male Fertility Evaluation

Fertility evaluation should not focus only on the female partner. Male factors can also contribute to difficulty conceiving.

When clinically appropriate, the male partner may be advised to undergo medical evaluation and a semen analysis or other investigations based on the clinical history.

Possible Investigations

The investigations required are different for every couple. Depending on the history and examination, your healthcare professional may consider:

  • Ovulation assessment
  • Pelvic ultrasound
  • Selected hormone testing
  • Thyroid testing when indicated
  • Prolactin testing when clinically appropriate
  • Ovarian reserve assessment when indicated
  • Semen analysis
  • Additional fertility investigations when required

Not every patient needs every test. Investigations should be selected according to the individual's history, examination and clinical needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an infertility evaluation?

It is a medical assessment of factors that may affect conception. Depending on the couple's history, it may include reproductive history, clinical assessment, selected tests, ultrasound and evaluation of male fertility factors.

When should a couple seek infertility evaluation?

Evaluation may be appropriate when a couple has difficulty conceiving despite regular unprotected intercourse, or earlier when there are specific fertility risk factors or concerns.

Is infertility evaluation needed for both partners?

Yes. Fertility difficulties can involve female factors, male factors, both partners or sometimes remain unexplained. Assessment of both partners may therefore be appropriate.

What tests may be advised?

Depending on the clinical situation, evaluation may include ovulation assessment, selected hormone tests, pelvic ultrasound and semen analysis, with additional investigations when clinically indicated.
