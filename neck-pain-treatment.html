Neck pain is commonly caused by poor posture, muscle strain, cervical spondylosis or disc-related problems. Early diagnosis and proper treatment can relieve pain and restore neck movement.

Common Symptoms

  • Neck pain
  • Neck stiffness
  • Pain radiating to the shoulder or arm
  • Headache
  • Numbness or tingling in the arms
  • Muscle spasm
  • Difficulty turning the neck
  • Shoulder pain associated with neck pain

Common Causes

  • Poor posture
  • Cervical spondylosis
  • Muscle strain
  • Cervical slipped disc
  • Whiplash injury
  • Degenerative spine disease
  • Prolonged mobile or computer use

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • X-ray Cervical Spine
  • MRI Cervical Spine (if required)
  • Neurological Examination
  • Blood Tests (selected patients)

Emergency: Seek immediate medical attention if neck pain follows major trauma, or is associated with arm weakness, loss of sensation or difficulty walking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can poor posture cause neck pain?

Yes. Long hours of computer work, mobile phone use and poor sitting posture are common causes of neck pain.

Can cervical spondylosis be treated without surgery?

Yes. Most patients improve with medicines, exercises, posture correction and lifestyle changes. Surgery is needed only in selected cases.

When should I consult a doctor?

Consult a doctor if pain persists for more than a few weeks, is associated with numbness, weakness or follows an injury.
