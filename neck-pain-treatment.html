Neck Pain Treatment in Behram

Neck pain is a common problem that may occur due to muscle strain, poor posture, prolonged mobile or computer use, cervical spondylosis, injury or other underlying conditions.

Common Causes of Neck Pain

  • Muscle strain or stiffness
  • Poor sitting or sleeping posture
  • Prolonged mobile or computer use
  • Cervical spondylosis
  • Neck injury or sports-related injury
  • Disc-related problems
  • Stress and muscle tension

Symptoms

  • Pain or stiffness in the neck
  • Difficulty moving the neck
  • Pain extending towards the shoulder or arm
  • Headache associated with neck stiffness
  • Tingling or numbness in the arm or hand

Diagnosis

Treatment depends on the cause and severity of the symptoms. A clinical examination is usually important. Depending on the presentation, the doctor may advise investigations such as X-ray or other imaging when clinically required.

Neck Pain Treatment

Treatment may include medicines for pain and inflammation when appropriate, activity modification, posture correction, stretching and strengthening exercises, physiotherapy, and other treatment depending on the underlying cause.

When to Seek Medical Attention

Seek medical evaluation if neck pain is severe, persistent, follows an injury, or is associated with weakness, numbness, difficulty walking, fever, severe headache or other concerning symptoms.
