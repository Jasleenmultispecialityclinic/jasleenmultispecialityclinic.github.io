How to Choose the Best Sexologist in Punjab

When searching for a sexologist or sexual health specialist in Punjab, it is important to look beyond online rankings. Sexual health concerns can have physical, psychological, relationship-related or medication-related causes.

Depending on the problem, appropriate care may involve a physician, urologist, gynecologist, psychiatrist, psychologist, counsellor or another appropriately trained healthcare professional.

Important: The term "best sexologist" is not a universal medical ranking. Choose a qualified healthcare professional whose training and clinical experience are appropriate for your particular concern.

Common Sexual Health Concerns

Erectile Difficulties

Difficulty getting or maintaining an erection can have vascular, hormonal, neurological, medication-related or psychological causes.

Premature Ejaculation

Premature ejaculation is a common sexual concern and can be evaluated with consideration of symptoms, relationship factors and medical history.

Low Sexual Desire

Reduced sexual desire may be associated with stress, relationship factors, medications, hormonal problems or other health conditions.

Pain During Intercourse

Pain during sexual activity may have gynecological, urological, musculoskeletal, hormonal or psychological causes.

Sexual Performance Anxiety

Anxiety and stress can affect sexual function. Appropriate counselling or medical evaluation may be useful depending on the situation.

Female Sexual Health

Women may experience low desire, painful intercourse, difficulty with arousal or orgasm and other concerns requiring appropriate evaluation.

Relationship & Intimacy Concerns

Communication, stress, relationship difficulties and psychological factors can sometimes contribute to sexual problems.

Sexual Health After Illness

Diabetes, cardiovascular disease, hormonal disorders and some medicines can affect sexual function and may require medical review.

When Should You Consult a Sexual Health Specialist?

  • A sexual problem is persistent or repeatedly occurring.
  • Erectile difficulties are affecting sexual activity.
  • Ejaculation occurs earlier than desired and causes distress.
  • Sexual desire has significantly changed.
  • Sexual activity causes pain.
  • There is anxiety or distress related to sexual performance.
  • A medical condition or medication may be affecting sexual function.
  • You or your partner are experiencing ongoing sexual difficulties.

What May Be Evaluated During a Sexual Health Consultation?

The evaluation depends on the symptoms and may include a detailed medical and sexual history. A healthcare professional may also review medications, lifestyle factors, psychological factors and relevant medical conditions.

When clinically indicated, investigations may be recommended to look for possible underlying causes. Treatment should be based on the individual assessment rather than a one-size-fits-all medicine.

Avoid self-medication: Medicines marketed online for sexual performance may not be appropriate for everyone and can interact with other medicines or underlying health conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a sexologist?

Sexology deals with human sexuality and sexual health. Depending on their training, healthcare professionals working in sexual health may address sexual dysfunction, psychological factors, relationship concerns and other sexual health problems.

What problems can sexual health specialists evaluate?

Common concerns include erectile difficulties, premature ejaculation, low sexual desire, painful intercourse, performance anxiety and other sexual dysfunctions.

Should women also consult a sexual health specialist?

Yes. Sexual health concerns can affect women as well as men. Depending on the symptoms, a gynecologist, sexual health specialist, psychologist or another appropriately trained professional may be appropriate.

Can stress cause sexual problems?

Stress, anxiety and relationship difficulties can contribute to sexual problems. Medical causes should also be considered when symptoms are persistent.

Is sexual health consultation confidential?

Sexual health concerns are sensitive medical issues. Patients should choose a professional healthcare setting where privacy and appropriate medical confidentiality are respected.
