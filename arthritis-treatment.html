Arthritis is a common condition that causes joint pain, stiffness and reduced movement. Early diagnosis and treatment help reduce pain and improve mobility.

Common Symptoms

  • Joint pain
  • Morning stiffness
  • Joint swelling
  • Difficulty walking
  • Reduced movement
  • Cracking sounds in joints
  • Weakness around joints

Common Types

  • Osteoarthritis
  • Rheumatoid Arthritis
  • Gout
  • Post-traumatic Arthritis

Diagnosis

  • Clinical Examination
  • X-ray
  • Blood Tests
  • ESR & CRP
  • Rheumatoid Factor (when indicated)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can arthritis be cured?

Many forms of arthritis cannot be cured, but symptoms can often be managed effectively with medicines, exercise and lifestyle changes.

Can exercise help arthritis?

Yes. Regular low-impact exercise helps improve joint movement and muscle strength.

When should I consult a doctor?

If you have persistent joint pain, swelling or morning stiffness lasting more than a few weeks, seek medical evaluation.
