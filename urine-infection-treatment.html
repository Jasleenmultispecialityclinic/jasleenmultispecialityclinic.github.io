What Is a Urinary Tract Infection?

A urinary tract infection (UTI) is an infection affecting part of the urinary system. UTIs can involve the bladder or other parts of the urinary tract and may require medical evaluation depending on the symptoms and severity.

Common Symptoms of UTI

  • Burning or pain while urinating
  • Frequent urge to urinate
  • Passing small amounts of urine frequently
  • Lower abdominal discomfort
  • Cloudy or unusually strong-smelling urine
  • Blood in the urine in some cases

When Infection May Affect the Kidneys

Fever, chills, pain in the side or back, nausea or vomiting along with urinary symptoms may indicate a more significant urinary infection and requires prompt medical assessment.

How Is a Urine Infection Diagnosed?

Diagnosis depends on the symptoms and clinical assessment. A doctor may recommend urine testing when appropriate. Depending on the situation, a urine routine examination and urine culture may be considered.

Urine Infection Treatment

Treatment depends on the likely cause, severity of symptoms and clinical findings. When a bacterial infection is suspected or confirmed, a doctor may prescribe an appropriate antibiotic. Treatment should be selected according to the individual patient and, when indicated, urine culture results.

Do not start antibiotics without appropriate medical advice, and do not stop a prescribed course early unless your doctor advises you to do so.

Women and Urinary Infections

Urinary infections are common in women. Recurrent urinary symptoms may require evaluation for contributing factors and appropriate investigations.

Recurrent Urine Infections

If urinary infections keep coming back, medical evaluation may be needed to identify possible contributing factors. Depending on the history, your doctor may recommend urine culture or other investigations.

When Should You Seek Medical Attention?

  • Fever or chills with urinary symptoms
  • Side or back pain
  • Blood in urine
  • Repeated urinary infections
  • Persistent symptoms despite treatment
  • Vomiting or significant weakness
  • Urinary symptoms during pregnancy
