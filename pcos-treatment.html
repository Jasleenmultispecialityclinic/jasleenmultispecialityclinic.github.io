PCOS Treatment

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a common hormonal condition affecting many women. It may cause irregular periods, weight gain, acne, unwanted facial hair, difficulty in pregnancy and hormonal imbalance. Early diagnosis and appropriate treatment can help manage symptoms and reduce long-term health risks.

Common Symptoms of PCOS

  • Irregular or missed periods
  • Weight gain
  • Acne
  • Excess facial or body hair
  • Difficulty in pregnancy
  • Hair fall
  • Dark skin around neck or underarms

Treatment Available

Treatment depends on your symptoms, age and future pregnancy plans. It may include lifestyle changes, weight management, medications to regulate periods, ovulation support when pregnancy is desired and regular follow-up with your doctor.

When Should You Consult a Doctor?

If your periods are irregular, you have excessive facial hair, acne, unexplained weight gain or difficulty becoming pregnant, consult a qualified healthcare professional for evaluation and appropriate treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can PCOS be cured?

PCOS is a long-term hormonal condition. Although it cannot always be permanently cured, its symptoms can usually be managed effectively with lifestyle changes, appropriate treatment and regular follow-up.

Can women with PCOS become pregnant?

Yes. Many women with PCOS are able to conceive naturally or with medical support, depending on their individual condition.

Does weight loss help in PCOS?

For women who are overweight, even modest weight loss may improve menstrual regularity, hormone balance and overall health.
