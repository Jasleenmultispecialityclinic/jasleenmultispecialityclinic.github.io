Common Sexual Health Concerns

Erectile Difficulties

Difficulty getting or maintaining an erection can have several causes, including vascular, hormonal, neurological, medication-related and psychological factors.

Premature Ejaculation

Premature ejaculation is a common sexual concern that may cause personal or relationship distress and can be evaluated professionally.

Low Sexual Desire

Reduced sexual desire can be associated with stress, relationship factors, medications, hormonal problems or other medical conditions.

Pain During Sexual Activity

Pain during intercourse may have gynecological, urological, hormonal, musculoskeletal or psychological causes.

Performance Anxiety

Stress and anxiety can affect sexual performance. Appropriate medical or psychological support may help depending on the cause.

Female Sexual Health

Women may experience low desire, painful intercourse, difficulty with arousal or orgasm and other sexual health concerns.

Relationship & Intimacy Concerns

Relationship difficulties, communication problems and emotional stress can sometimes contribute to sexual difficulties.

Sexual Problems Associated With Medical Conditions

Diabetes, cardiovascular disease, hormonal disorders and some medicines can affect sexual function and may require medical review.

When Should You Consult a Sexual Health Professional?

  • A sexual problem is persistent or repeatedly occurring.
  • Erectile difficulties are affecting sexual activity.
  • Ejaculation occurs earlier than desired and causes distress.
  • Sexual desire has significantly changed.
  • Sexual activity causes pain.
  • Performance anxiety is affecting sexual life.
  • A medical condition or medicine may be affecting sexual function.
  • You or your partner are experiencing ongoing sexual difficulties.

What Happens During a Sexual Health Consultation?

A consultation usually begins with a discussion of the symptoms, duration, medical history and relevant sexual health concerns. Depending on the symptoms, the healthcare professional may also review medications, lifestyle factors, psychological factors and existing medical conditions.

Additional investigations may be recommended when clinically indicated. The evaluation should be individualized rather than based on a standard medicine or supplement for everyone.

Avoid self-medication: Sexual-performance medicines and supplements purchased online may not be suitable for everyone and can interact with medicines or underlying health conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a sexologist?

Sexology is the study and clinical field related to human sexuality and sexual health. Depending on their training, healthcare professionals may address sexual dysfunction, psychological factors, relationship concerns and other sexual health problems.

What problems can a sexual health professional evaluate?

Common concerns include erectile difficulties, premature ejaculation, low sexual desire, painful intercourse, performance anxiety and other sexual dysfunctions.

Can women consult a sexual health professional?

Yes. Sexual health concerns can affect women and men. Depending on the symptoms, a gynecologist, sexual health specialist, psychologist or another appropriately trained professional may be suitable.

Can stress affect sexual function?

Stress and anxiety can contribute to sexual difficulties. Persistent symptoms should also be assessed for possible medical causes.

Is sexual health consultation confidential?

Sexual health concerns are sensitive medical issues. Patients should choose a professional healthcare setting where privacy and appropriate medical confidentiality are respected.
