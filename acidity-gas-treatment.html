Acidity, Gas & Indigestion Consultation

Acidity, heartburn, gas and bloating are common digestive complaints. They may occur occasionally after meals or may become persistent and recurrent.

Persistent digestive symptoms can have several possible causes. At Jasleen Multi-Speciality Clinic in Behram, your symptoms and medical history can be assessed to determine whether further evaluation is needed.

Common Symptoms

  • Heartburn or burning sensation in the chest
  • Sour or acidic taste in the mouth
  • Regurgitation of food or stomach contents
  • Belching
  • Abdominal bloating
  • Upper abdominal discomfort
  • Indigestion or feeling unusually full after meals
  • Nausea in some cases

Common Causes of Acidity & Gas

Digestive symptoms can have many causes. Depending on the individual, possible contributing factors may include:

  • Large or heavy meals
  • Eating too quickly
  • Certain foods or beverages
  • Overeating or irregular meal patterns
  • Obesity or overweight
  • Constipation
  • Some medicines
  • Acid reflux / GERD
  • Functional digestive disorders
  • Other gastrointestinal conditions

Simple Lifestyle Measures

For people with occasional reflux or indigestion, some lifestyle changes may help reduce symptoms.

  • Eat smaller meals if large meals trigger symptoms.
  • Avoid lying down immediately after eating.
  • Identify foods that consistently trigger your symptoms.
  • Maintain a healthy weight where appropriate.
  • Stay physically active.
  • Avoid smoking.
  • Limit alcohol if you consume it.
  • Maintain regular meal patterns.

Acidity and Acid Reflux

Acid reflux occurs when stomach contents move back into the esophagus. It can cause heartburn, sour taste or regurgitation.

When reflux symptoms occur frequently or interfere with daily life, medical evaluation may be appropriate.

Gas and Bloating

Gas and bloating can occur for many reasons and are not always caused by excess stomach acid.

Dietary factors, constipation, food intolerance, functional bowel disorders and other gastrointestinal conditions can contribute to bloating and gas.

When Further Evaluation May Be Needed

Your doctor may recommend additional evaluation when symptoms are persistent, recurrent, severe or associated with other concerning features.

Depending on the clinical situation, investigations may include blood tests, stool tests, testing for specific infections or gastrointestinal evaluation.

Acidity and H. pylori

Helicobacter pylori is a type of bacteria that can infect the stomach and is associated with peptic ulcer disease and some cases of dyspepsia.

Testing and treatment for H. pylori should be considered based on the individual clinical situation and appropriate medical guidance.

When Should You See a Doctor?

Medical consultation is recommended if acidity, gas or indigestion keeps returning, becomes severe or does not improve with appropriate lifestyle measures.

Seek prompt medical attention for symptoms such as difficulty swallowing, persistent vomiting, vomiting blood, black stools, unexplained weight loss or severe chest or abdominal pain.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are common symptoms of acidity and gas?

Common symptoms may include heartburn, sour taste or regurgitation, belching, abdominal bloating, upper abdominal discomfort and indigestion.

What can cause frequent acidity?

Frequent symptoms may be related to acid reflux, certain foods, large meals, obesity, some medicines and other digestive conditions.

Can gas and bloating have causes other than acidity?

Yes. Gas and bloating can occur with dietary factors, constipation, food intolerance, functional digestive disorders and other gastrointestinal conditions.

When should acidity be medically evaluated?

Persistent or frequently recurring symptoms, difficulty swallowing, unexplained weight loss, vomiting, gastrointestinal bleeding or severe symptoms should be medically evaluated.
