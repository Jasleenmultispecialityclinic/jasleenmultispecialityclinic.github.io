Frequently Asked Questions

When should I start ANC checkups?

As soon as pregnancy is confirmed, preferably within the first trimester.

How often are ANC visits required?

The number of visits depends on your pregnancy stage and any associated medical conditions.

Why are blood tests important?

They help detect anemia, diabetes, infections and thyroid disorders during pregnancy.

Can high blood pressure affect pregnancy?

Yes. High blood pressure requires regular monitoring to reduce complications.

Why is ultrasound important?

Ultrasound helps monitor fetal growth, development and pregnancy well-being.
