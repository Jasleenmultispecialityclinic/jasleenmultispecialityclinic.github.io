Menstrual Problems & Women's Health Consultation

Menstrual cycles can vary from person to person, but significant changes in the timing, duration, amount of bleeding or level of pain may need medical evaluation.

At Jasleen Multi-Speciality Clinic in Behram, women can discuss changes in their menstrual cycles and receive appropriate clinical evaluation based on their symptoms, medical history and reproductive goals.

Common Menstrual Problems

  • Irregular periods
  • Delayed or infrequent periods
  • Very frequent periods
  • Heavy menstrual bleeding
  • Very painful periods
  • Periods lasting unusually long
  • Bleeding between periods
  • Changes in periods after starting or stopping medicines
  • Periods stopping unexpectedly

Causes of Irregular Periods

Menstrual irregularity can have many possible causes. Depending on the individual situation, your doctor may consider:

  • Pregnancy
  • PCOS / PCOD
  • Thyroid disorders
  • Changes in body weight
  • Stress and significant lifestyle changes
  • Hormonal conditions
  • Certain medicines
  • Perimenopause or other reproductive changes
  • Other gynecological conditions

Heavy Menstrual Bleeding

Heavy menstrual bleeding can sometimes lead to iron deficiency or anemia. The cause may vary and can include hormonal problems, fibroids, certain uterine conditions, medications and other medical causes.

If bleeding is repeatedly heavy, prolonged or associated with weakness, dizziness, breathlessness or other concerning symptoms, medical evaluation is important.

Painful Periods

Period pain is common, but severe or progressively worsening pain should not simply be ignored. Depending on the symptoms, your doctor may evaluate for conditions such as endometriosis, adenomyosis, fibroids or other gynecological causes.

Menstrual Problems and PCOS

PCOS can cause irregular or infrequent periods in some women because ovulation may not occur regularly. Other features such as acne, excess facial or body hair and weight-related metabolic problems may also occur.

Possible Evaluation

The tests needed depend on age, symptoms, pregnancy possibility, menstrual history and clinical examination. Depending on the situation, evaluation may include:

  • Pregnancy testing when appropriate
  • Complete blood count
  • Thyroid testing
  • Selected hormonal tests
  • Pelvic ultrasound
  • Other investigations when clinically indicated

Not every woman needs all of these tests. Your doctor will select investigations based on the individual clinical situation.

When Should You Consult a Doctor?

Consider medical evaluation if your periods become consistently irregular, very heavy, unusually painful, persistently prolonged or stop unexpectedly.

Urgent medical attention may be necessary for very heavy bleeding with fainting, severe dizziness, marked weakness, shortness of breath or other signs of significant blood loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are common menstrual problems?

Common concerns include irregular periods, very heavy bleeding, painful periods, unusually frequent or infrequent periods and periods that stop unexpectedly.

What can cause irregular periods?

Possible causes include pregnancy, PCOS, thyroid disorders, changes in weight, stress, certain medicines and other hormonal or reproductive conditions.

When should I see a doctor for heavy periods?

Medical evaluation is appropriate when bleeding is unusually heavy, repeatedly lasts longer than expected or causes weakness, dizziness or other concerning symptoms.

Can PCOS cause irregular periods?

Yes. PCOS can be associated with irregular or infrequent ovulation and menstrual cycles in some women.
