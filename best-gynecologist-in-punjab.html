What to Look for in a Good Gynecologist

Recognized Qualifications

Check that the doctor has appropriate recognized medical qualifications and relevant training in obstetrics and gynecology.

Relevant Experience

Experience related to your particular condition can be important, especially for long-standing or complex women's health problems.

Clear Communication

A comfortable consultation allows patients to discuss sensitive women's health symptoms and concerns openly.

Follow-up Care

Good medical care includes appropriate follow-up and referral for investigations or specialist treatment when required.

When Should You Visit a Gynecologist?

  • Periods are repeatedly irregular.
  • Periods are unusually heavy or prolonged.
  • Period pain is severe or affecting daily life.
  • You have persistent pelvic or lower abdominal pain.
  • You have abnormal vaginal discharge, itching or burning.
  • You are planning a pregnancy.
  • You are having difficulty conceiving.
  • You need antenatal or pregnancy care.
  • You are experiencing menopause-related symptoms.
  • You have concerns about PCOS, fibroids, ovarian cysts or endometriosis.

Severe abdominal pain, very heavy bleeding, fainting, severe weakness or pregnancy-related emergencies require urgent medical assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find the best gynecologist in Punjab?

Consider recognized qualifications, relevant experience, communication, accessibility, available investigations and follow-up care. Your choice should also be based on the specific health concern you need evaluated.

Can a gynecologist treat PCOS?

Yes. A gynecologist can evaluate menstrual and hormonal symptoms associated with PCOS and recommend appropriate assessment and management.

When should I see a gynecologist for irregular periods?

If periods remain consistently irregular, very heavy, prolonged or painful, a gynecological consultation can help identify possible causes.

Can gynecologists help with infertility?

Yes. Infertility evaluation may involve assessment of both partners and investigations selected according to the clinical history.

Can I consult a gynecologist for pregnancy care?

Yes. Pregnancy and antenatal care are important parts of obstetrics and gynecology practice.
